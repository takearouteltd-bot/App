Decision needed — are you the operator or an agent? Under the Private Hire Vehicles (London) Act 1998 and the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976, whoever accepts a private hire booking must hold an operator's licence, and following the 2021 Uber London ruling an operator in London contracts with the passenger directly rather than as the driver's agent. These terms are written on the basis that TAKE A ROUTE MOBILITY LTD accepts the booking and contracts with the passenger, which is the position that fits that case law. If your licensing position is different, sections 3, 5 and 14 all need rewriting. Take advice on this.

3. What the service is

TakeARoute is an app for booking journeys in licensed private hire vehicles, driven by licensed drivers. When you book, we accept the booking and arrange for a driver to carry out the journey.

Every driver on the platform must hold a current private hire driver licence, drive a licensed vehicle, and hold hire and reward insurance and a valid MOT. We check these documents before approving a driver and again when they are due to expire. Drivers are self-employed and are not our employees.

We cannot guarantee that a driver will always be available, and availability depends on how many drivers are online near you at the time.

Check this matches the business. The website currently advertises city-to-city travel, delivery, and pricing that the passenger and driver "agree on". The app does none of those three — it books point-to-point rides at a metered fare that we calculate and quote up front. Either the app needs to catch up with the website or the website needs to match the app, and these terms should describe whichever is true.

4. Booking a ride

  1. Enter where you are going. We show you the vehicle classes available and the fare for each.
  2. Choose a class and confirm. The fare shown is the fare for that journey.
  3. We offer the job to suitable drivers near you. A driver accepts it.
  4. You can follow the driver to your pickup, message them, or call them without either of you seeing the other's number.

Vehicle classes differ by size and standard of vehicle. A booking is only offered to drivers whose vehicle qualifies for the class you chose — an Executive booking is never given to a standard vehicle. Where a driver's vehicle exceeds the class you booked, they may still pick you up, and you pay the fare for the class you booked.

5. Fares and how they are worked out

The fare is calculated before you book and is made up of:

  • a base fare;
  • a distance charge for the estimated journey distance;
  • a time charge for the estimated journey time;
  • a multiplier for the vehicle class you chose;
  • any promotional discount you have applied; and
  • VAT at the applicable rate.

A minimum fare applies to short journeys. The current rates are always shown in the app before you confirm, and the full breakdown is available on the fare screen and on your receipt.

The fare you are quoted is what you pay, with two exceptions: waiting time (section 7), and any change you ask the driver to make to the journey, which may be re-priced.

We may change our rates. A change never affects a booking you have already confirmed.

6. Paying

  • You must have a valid payment card saved before you can book.
  • When a driver accepts your booking, we ask your bank to hold the quoted fare on your card. This is not a charge.
  • When the trip finishes, we take the final amount, including any waiting time.
  • If the trip is cancelled, the hold is released. Your bank decides how quickly that shows on your statement — usually a few working days.
  • A receipt is emailed to you once payment is taken. You can email yourself another copy from the trip in your trip history.

Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach our servers. If a payment fails we may ask you to settle it before booking again.

7. Waiting time

Your driver waits a set number of minutes free of charge from the moment they arrive at the pickup point. After that, waiting is charged per minute, up to a maximum. The free period, the per-minute rate and the maximum are all shown in the app, and the terms that applied when you booked are the terms you are charged under.

The app shows the waiting clock to both of you while it runs, so there are no surprises. If you do not turn up at all, the driver may cancel and you may be charged as set out in section 8.

9. Behaviour and safety

You agree that you will not:

  • damage or soil a vehicle;
  • ask a driver to break the law, including speeding or carrying more passengers than the vehicle is licensed for;

If you damage or soil a vehicle, we may charge you the reasonable cost of cleaning or repair, with evidence provided to you.

A consistently low rating, or a serious report, may lead to an account being suspended or closed. You can also ask us never to match you with a particular driver again, from the trip in your trip history.

12. Terms for drivers

Your status

You are self-employed. You are not our employee or worker, you choose when to go online, and you are free to decline any job. You are responsible for your own tax and National Insurance, and for telling HMRC about your earnings.

Employment status is contested territory. A written label does not settle how a tribunal would classify a driver — what matters is the substance of the arrangement. Take advice before relying on this section.

What you must keep in place

  • A current private hire driver licence, and a licensed vehicle.
  • Hire and reward insurance covering you for carrying passengers.
  • A valid MOT, and a DBS certificate.
  • The right to work in the UK.

Upload these before you start and replace them before they expire. The app warns you in advance. If a document expires, you cannot go online until it is replaced. Driving without valid licensing or insurance is a criminal offence, and it is your responsibility, not ours.

Membership and what you earn

  • You pay a monthly membership fee to use the platform. The current amount is shown on the Membership screen.
  • We take no commission. The full fare for every trip is credited to your wallet.
  • The membership fee comes out of your wallet. If your balance is short, it is taken automatically once you have earned enough, and your membership is suspended until then.
  • You can request a payout once your balance reaches the minimum shown on the withdrawal screen. We check payouts before sending them, normally within one to two working days.
  • Bank details can only be changed by contacting us, for your protection.

Jobs

Jobs are offered to you based on how near you are to the pickup and whether your vehicle qualifies for the class booked. Declining jobs does not penalise you. If you accept a job and then cannot do it, hand it back promptly so another driver can take it — that job will not be offered to you again.

There is a maximum shift length for safety. When you reach it you are taken offline and must take a break. If you are on a trip, you finish that trip first.
