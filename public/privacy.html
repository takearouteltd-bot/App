2. What we collect

Everyone with an account

  • Who you are: name, email address, mobile number, and a profile photo if you add one.
  • How you signed in: whether you used a phone number or an email address, and the date you created the account.
  • Your device: a push notification token for each phone you sign in on, and the platform (iOS or Android). We use this to send you trip updates. Removing notification permission, or turning notifications off in the app, deletes the token.
  • Emergency contact: if you add one, their name, phone number and relationship to you. We only show this to you, inside the app's safety sheet. We do not contact them ourselves.

Passengers

  • Trips: pickup and drop-off addresses and coordinates, the route distance and duration, the vehicle class you chose, the fare and how it was made up, timestamps for each stage, and whether the trip completed or was cancelled.
  • Places you save: home, work and any other saved addresses, and destinations you have recently searched for.
  • Payment: a Stripe customer reference, and the card brand, last four digits, expiry month and year, and the cardholder name. See section 7 — we never hold your full card number.
  • Location while booking: your device location when you open the app to set a pickup point. See section 4.
  • Ratings: the star ratings you give drivers, and the ratings drivers give you.
  • Drivers you have asked not to be matched with, and the driver's name and registration at the time you asked.

Drivers

Everything above, and because driving passengers for hire is a licensed activity:

  • Identity: first and last name, date of birth, home address, and a verification selfie.
  • National Insurance number.
  • Licensing and compliance documents, and their expiry dates: driving licence (front and back), private hire driver licence, vehicle licence, hire and reward insurance certificate, MOT certificate, and DBS certificate.
  • Vehicle: make and model, year, colour, registration number, and the vehicle class you are approved to drive.
  • Bank details for payouts: account holder name, sort code and account number.
  • Live location while you are online. See section 4 — this is the most significant thing we collect about drivers.
  • Working patterns: when you go online and offline, how long your shift has run, and which jobs you accepted, declined or handed back.
  • Earnings: your wallet balance, each trip's earnings, your membership payments, and your payout requests and their status.

When you contact us or use the app's messaging

  • In-trip chat: messages between you and the other person on a trip, and whether they have been read.
  • Reports: anything you report to us, the trip it relates to, and the conversation with our support team afterwards.
  • Masked calls: a record that a call was placed, who started it, which trip it related to and when. See section 8.

4. Location data

Passengers

The app asks for your location when you open it, so it can set your pickup point and show nearby drivers. We record the pickup and drop-off of each trip as part of the trip record. We do not track a passenger's location in the background or between trips.

Drivers

While you are online, the app sends your location to us continuously — roughly every few seconds — along with your speed and the direction you are travelling.

We need this to offer you jobs near you, to show your passenger where you are, and to keep a record if something goes wrong on a trip. Your current position is visible to the passenger on a trip you have accepted, and to our operations team.

It stops when you go offline. You control this with the Go / Stop control on the driver home screen, and by withdrawing location permission in your phone settings — though you cannot receive jobs without it.

We hold your latest known position, not a continuous history of everywhere you have been. Each update replaces the last.

5. Criminal records and other sensitive data

To drive on TakeARoute you must supply a DBS certificate. A DBS certificate is criminal offence data under Article 10 of the UK GDPR, and we handle it under Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018 for the purpose of assessing your suitability to carry passengers.

Only the staff who assess and approve driver applications can see these documents. We do not use them for anything other than checking that you are licensed and eligible to drive, and we do not share them except as described in section 6.

Your verification selfie is used to check you are the person named on the licence. We do not run facial recognition on it and we do not use it to identify you anywhere else.

7. Payments and card details

Card payments are handled by Stripe. When you add a card, the card number, expiry and security code go directly from your phone to Stripe — they never reach our servers and we never see them. Stripe returns a token, and we store only the brand, the last four digits, the expiry and the cardholder name so you can recognise the card.

When a driver accepts your booking we ask Stripe to hold the estimated fare on your card. When the trip finishes we take the final amount, including any waiting time. If the trip is cancelled, the hold is released.

Drivers' bank details are stored so we can pay out earnings. They can only be changed by contacting us, not in the app.

8. Calls and messages

When you tap Call during a trip, we ask Twilio to ring your phone and then connect you to the other person. Both of you see a TakeARoute number rather than each other's. We do not store either phone number against the call. We keep a record that a call happened, who started it, which trip it was for and when, so we can look into it if there is a dispute.

In-trip chat messages are stored against the trip. Either of you can report a message to us, which sends it to our support team for review.

11. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete it. We may have to keep some records — a completed trip and its payment, for example — for the periods in section 9.
  • Restrict how we use it while a concern is being looked into.
  • Send it to another service in a portable format.
  • Stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests. You can object at any time and we will stop unless we have compelling grounds not to.
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on it, such as push notifications.

Email info@takearoute.co.uk and we will respond within one month. There is no charge.

We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone that have a legal or similarly significant effect. Driver applications, suspensions and payouts are all decided by a person.

12. Keeping data safe

Data is held in Google Cloud, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to driver documents, bank details and support conversations is limited to the staff who need it. Card numbers are never held by us. We review who has access, and we log administrative actions.

No system is completely secure. If a breach happens that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

13. Children

TakeARoute is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about children. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

14. Changes
